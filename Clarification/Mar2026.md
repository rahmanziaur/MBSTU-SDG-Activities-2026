
# Clarifications on Methodology Refinements and Significant Data Variances

This document outlines key methodological refinements and major year-on-year variances observed in the institutional data submission.

---

## 1. Staff Data Calculation (Full-Time Equivalent)

Following our inaugural submission last year, we have refined our data calculation process to provide a more comprehensive representation of our academic staff.

For the current submission cycle:

- Part-time faculty and research staff are included.
- All staff figures are calculated as **Full-Time Equivalents (FTE)**.

This methodological refinement resulted in **more than a 30% increase in staff-related metrics** compared to our previous submission.

---

## 2. Institutional Income

The **institutional income within the Computer Science subject area** has increased significantly (**exceeding 400%**) compared to the previous year.

This growth is mainly attributed to:

- Two major funding grants secured by  
  - **Department of Computer Science and Engineering**  
  - **Department of Information and Communication Technology**

---

## 3. Growth in Engineering Subject Area

The **Engineering subject area** experienced substantial growth in:

- Student enrollment
- Institutional income

This increase is primarily due to the **inauguration and first student intake of the Department of Mechanical Engineering**, which significantly expanded institutional capacity and revenue streams.
